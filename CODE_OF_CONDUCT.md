# Community code of conduct

TableIQ welcomes people with different backgrounds, identities and experience levels. Help make its issues, pull requests, discussions and project spaces useful and respectful.

- Be considerate. Critique ideas and behavior with concrete evidence; do not attack people.
- Ask questions in good faith, explain mistakes constructively and respect others' time.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, share sexual content directed at participants or repeatedly contact someone who has asked you to stop.
- Do not publish another person's private information, credentials or private messages without permission.
- Keep promotion relevant and clearly disclose affiliations.

These expectations apply in project-managed spaces and when someone is officially representing the project. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, restrict participation temporarily or permanently, and explain a decision when doing so would not expose private information. Good-faith disagreement with a technical decision is not a violation.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact route on [Rudresh Narwal's GitHub profile](https://github.com/RudreshNarwal). If a safe private route is unavailable, contact GitHub Support for platform abuse; do not open a public issue containing personal details. Security vulnerabilities have a separate process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Reports will be handled with care and shared only as needed to investigate/respond. Absolute confidentiality or an immediate response cannot be guaranteed. Reporters and subjects should avoid retaliation. A concern about a maintainer can be raised through GitHub's platform reporting process rather than contacting that maintainer publicly.
