Why this direction

The opening is a developer pain the viewer can recognize. The middle shows one useful database investigation. The two returned orders make the result concrete. MCP appears after the database client is understood.

Theme: brand Pearl #F1F6FB, Ledger Ink #0C1A2B and Lumen Deep #0B7480. Keep actual captured app colors intact.

Claim boundary: TablePlus documents a two-tab free-trial limit. DBeaver is also free and open source. “Built with Rust” is a fact; faster-than claims require matched benchmarks.
